# why-are-we-here
## Coming from a background in Design Management and creative Planning, I have always viewed design as an exercise in turning abstract concepts into tangible experiences.However, throughout my practice, I constantly hit an invisible celling: techical execution. 
## When designing my undergraduate capstone project which is an indie game,I lacked technical proficiency in game engines and 3D modeling. To deliver the project on time, I strategically worked around my limitations. I opted for 2D graphics over 3D, and built the game within RPG Maker instead of a custom engine. While this compromise allowed me to complete the assignment, it taught me a frustrating lesson is that when you do not understand the underlying technology of you medium, your tools end uo defining your creative boundaries rether than  your original vision.
